# Cover letter and written answers

- Use ONLY facts in the chosen resume: skills, projects, education, jobs, dates.
  Never invent experience, tools, employers, metrics, awards or personal details.
  If the posting asks for something the resume doesn't show, don't claim it; you may
  say he is keen to learn it.
- Education dates are in the resume; if the end date is in the past, describe the degree as completed.
- Connect 2–3 of his most relevant projects or experiences to specific needs in the
  posting. His current customer-facing job can show leadership, reliability and
  customer focus, using only what the resume says about it.
- 250–350 words, 4 short paragraphs, warm, confident, plain. Canadian spelling.
  No clichés ("I am writing to express my interest", "I am excited to apply").
  No placeholders.
- Start "Dear Hiring Manager," (or the named person). End "Sincerely," then
  "Vedant Prajapati" on the next line. Output only the letter text: no date or address
  block (letter.js adds the header).
- Short written answers on forms: 2–4 sentences, same rules, first person.
